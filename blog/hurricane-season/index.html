🌀 Hurricane Season Is Here: Are You Ready?

As we head into the start of hurricane season in Tampa Bay, now is the time to prepare before storms are on the radar.

A few quick essentials to check off your list:
🏠 Secure outdoor furniture and loose items around your home
🌳 Trim trees and remove weak or overhanging branches
🔦 Restock emergency kits with flashlights, batteries, water, and first aid supplies
📄 Review insurance policies and keep important documents in a safe, dry place
📱 Make sure you have backup charging options for your devices

And don’t forget the title side of things: make sure your home’s title and closing documents are organized and accessible. Having everything in order now helps reduce stress if you ever need to move quickly after a storm.

At Firm Title TB, we’re here to help protect what matters most, so you can focus on staying safe and prepared.

Stay ready, Tampa Bay.
