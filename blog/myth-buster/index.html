🧠 Title Myth Buster: “You always have to use the title company your agent recommends.”

Not true.

In Florida, you have the right to choose your own title company and that choice matters more than most people realize.

The title company plays a key role in protecting your transaction, reviewing ownership history, clearing liens, and ensuring a smooth closing. The goal is not just to close the deal but to close it correctly and securely.

At Firm Title TB, we believe clients deserve clarity, transparency, and confidence throughout the closing process, no matter how simple or complex the transaction.

Have questions about title or closing? We are here to help break it down.
