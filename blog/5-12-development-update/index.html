🚨 St. Petersburg Waterfront Development Update 🚨

⚓ Master Plan Proposed for the Port of St. Petersburg

A major long-term vision is taking shape for St. Pete’s waterfront, with plans to transform the Port of St. Petersburg into a vibrant mixed-use marine destination over the next 20 years.

📈 Key Highlights & Features
🔹 Estimated Project Cost: $158M–$270M (excluding design, soft costs, and future pricing)
🔹 Phased 20-Year Development Plan: Funding expected through grants and private partnerships
🔹 Four Acres of Waterfront Activation: Designed to enhance tourism, recreation, and marine operations
🔹 Harborside Park & Restaurant: New public gathering spaces along the waterfront
🔹 Cruise Terminal Pier: Expanded tourism and cruise opportunities for the region
🔹 Marine Operations Center: Supporting the city’s growing marine infrastructure
🔹 Superyacht Marina & Repair Facility: Docks for recreational vessels alongside maintenance and overhaul capabilities

✨ Why This Matters for Tampa Bay Growth
Projects like this continue to position St. Petersburg and the greater Tampa Bay region as a premier waterfront destination for tourism, marine industry, and economic development. Long-term infrastructure investments along the waterfront can create significant impacts on local business growth, property values, and regional investment opportunities.
