Buyer's Closing Cost Calculator

Estimate your total closing costs as a home buyer in Tampa Bay, Florida. This calculator includes all standard Florida buyer closing costs:

  • Title Insurance — Owner's and lender's title insurance premiums based on Florida promulgated rates
  • Documentary Stamps — Florida documentary stamp tax on the deed
  • Intangible Tax — Florida intangible tax on the mortgage
  • Recording Fees — Hillsborough and Pinellas county recording fees
  • Settlement Fee — Title company closing/settlement charges
  • Lien Search — Municipal and county lien search fees
  • Survey — Property survey costs
